# Homework 1 — Your Live Site

**Google Classroom assignment**
**Points:** 10
**Due:** evening before Day 2
**Submit:** your live URL, plus short written answers

---

## Part 1 — Extend your site (4 points)

Add to your live page:

- A list of 3 things you want to be able to build after this course
- Any change to the colours or layout

Push the change and confirm it appears on the live URL.

## Part 2 — What is actually happening (6 points)

Answer in your own words. Short answers are fine — 2 or 3 sentences each.
**Do not ask the agent to answer these for you.** You may ask it to explain the topic,
then write your own answer.

1. What is the difference between `localhost:3000` and your live URL?
2. When your friend opens your site, where is the code running — on their computer or somewhere else?
3. What did GitHub do in this process? What did Railway do? They are not the same thing.
4. You changed one line and the live site updated. List the steps that happened between your change and the site updating.

## Done when

- [ ] Your live URL shows the new list
- [ ] You have written answers to all 4 questions
- [ ] You did not copy the agent's answer

## Submit

1. Your live live URL
2. Your 4 answers, typed into the Classroom submission box

## Note

Question 4 is the important one. If you cannot list the steps, you do not yet know what
you built — and that is exactly what day 2 fixes.
