# Day 6 — Capstone

**Goal:** Ship **your own** mini-app, with 3 real users, by end of class. Explain it out loud, with no AI open.

---

## Today's rules

1. **One problem. One page. One table.** No scope creep.
2. **Live URL by minute 85** (60 min build + 25 min buffer).
3. **3 real signups by end of class.**
4. **4-minute oral exam with the instructor** — no AI, no notes.

If any of these fails, you have not completed Course 0.

---

## Step 1 — Scope your capstone (10 min)

Write this sentence on paper, filling the blanks:

> **"My capstone mini-app is for ______ to ______. They will use it from ______ when they ______."**

Example:

> *"My capstone mini-app is for my aunt to track her monthly rotating-savings-group contributions. She will use it from her phone on the 1st and 15th of each month."*

**Show the instructor.** They will reject if:
- The sentence has "and" in it (you have 2 features — cut to 1)
- You name "users" instead of a specific person or role
- You can't fit it in 60 minutes

Iterate until approved.

---

## Step 2 — Build and ship (60 min)

Use whichever tool suits you — v0, Lovable, Bolt, Claude artifact. **You know the pattern by now.**

Rules:
- Form + list is often enough
- One Supabase table
- Add signup/login if the user needs their own data; otherwise skip auth
- Deploy to a URL anyone can open

**At minute 50, STOP adding things. Deploy what you have.** You can iterate later.

---

## Step 3 — Get 3 real users (10 min)

Right now, in class:

1. Message 3 real people (friends, family — NOT students in this cohort)
2. Send them the URL
3. Ask them to **sign up and add 1 row**

If they can't do it in 10 min, text them, keep going. Show the instructor your Supabase Authentication → Users table.

---

## Step 4 — Final Oral (4 min per student)

The instructor will call you up. **Close your laptop. No notes. No AI.**

You will be asked some of these:

1. **What does your mini-app do? Who is it for?**
2. **Walk me through what happens when a user signs up.** Where does the data go?
3. **Walk me through what happens when a user adds a row.** Which table? Which columns?
4. **Show me your Supabase table.** Name the columns and tell me what each one stores.
5. **Point at a part of the UI and explain it.**
6. **What would you build next, and why?**

**You don't need code vocabulary.** You need to know **your own app** — the user, the data, the flow.

---

## Done when

- [ ] Scope approved by instructor
- [ ] Live URL with your capstone
- [ ] Supabase shows **3 real signups** (not yourself, not another student in this cohort)
- [ ] You answered 4 minutes of oral questions, no AI, no notes
- [ ] You passed criterion 2 (instructor confirms)

---

## What success looks like after class

You walk out with:

- A URL you can put in your WhatsApp status
- A real app with real users
- The ability to explain it to anyone — including a potential employer or customer

**That's Course 0 complete.** You're a builder. Not a developer, not an engineer — a builder.

If you want to go further:
- **Course 1** — learn to read the code AI gives you, direct it more precisely, verify it
- **Course 2** — integrations (payments, email, APIs)
- **Course 3** — architecture (when apps get bigger)
- **Course 4** — scale (when they get busier)

Ask the instructor about Course 1 if you want to continue.
