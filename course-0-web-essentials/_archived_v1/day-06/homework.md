# Day 6 Homework (Post-Course) — Keep Your Mini-App Alive for 1 Week

## Why this homework exists

Shipping is the beginning, not the end. This homework proves you can keep a thing alive after the course ends. **This is what being a builder actually feels like** — small daily tending, not big one-time launches.

Submit this a week after Day 6. It is your final course deliverable.

---

## What to do over 1 week (~4 hours total)

### Part 1 — Promote your mini-app (1 hour, spread across the week)

Get more real users:
- Post the URL on your Facebook / WhatsApp status
- Share with 5 more friends
- If appropriate: show it to the person you built it for and ask them to use it for real

**Goal: 10 total signups** (3 from class + 7 more). If you fall short, that's a data point too.

### Part 2 — Watch for problems (ongoing)

Over the week:
- Does the app break?
- Do users ask you questions?
- Do you notice something you'd change?

Write down everything in a running list.

### Part 3 — Fix one bug, add one small improvement (2 hours)

Based on real user behavior:
- Fix **one bug** that showed up
- Add **one small improvement** (not a big feature — a polish)

Example: "The date picker was confusing. Changed to a dropdown with 'today' and 'yesterday'."

Deploy the update.

### Part 4 — Collect feedback from 3 users (30 min)

Message 3 users and ask:
1. "Did you actually use the app this week?"
2. "What made it useful or not useful?"
3. "Would you use it again next week?"

### Part 5 — Final written reflection (30 min)

Submit:

**1. Final capstone URL** (should still work)

**2. Supabase screenshot** — Authentication → Users, showing total signups

**3. User quotes** — what 3 users said about using it for real

**4. Written reflection** (8-12 sentences):

- How many real users did you get?
- Did anyone actually use it, or did they just sign up and leave?
- What one thing did you fix during the week?
- What one thing did you add during the week?
- What's the biggest difference between "it works" (end of Day 6) and "people actually use it" (today)?
- Will you keep this mini-app alive? Why / why not?
- What would you build next?
- If you could redo Course 0, what would you do differently?

Burmese or English, your choice.

## Rules

1. **Don't rush this.** Spread it across the week. The point is to feel what maintenance is like.
2. **If you abandoned the mini-app** (didn't log in all week), say so in the reflection — that's an honest data point.
3. **If no users came back after signing up**, write about that too. It's useful information.
4. **This is your last homework in Course 0.** If you pass, you've completed the course.
