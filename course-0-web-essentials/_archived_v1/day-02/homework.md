# Day 2 Homework — Interview 1 Person, Ship Their Mini-App

## Why this homework exists

Guessing what someone needs almost never works. **Asking** them does. This homework trains the one skill every builder needs: **shut up and ask**.

---

## What to do (3-4 hours)

### Part 1 — Pick someone and interview them (30 min)

Pick a real person. **Different from your Day 1 homework person.** Could be:

- A shopkeeper near your home
- A family member who runs any kind of business or activity
- A friend who complains about something specific they do manually

Ask them these questions. Write down the answers.

1. "What is one thing you do every day that takes you time or you forget to do?"
2. "How do you do it now?" (phone? paper? Excel? by memory?)
3. "If I made you a simple page on your phone to help with this, what should it do?"
4. "What would you NOT want it to do?" (this is the most useful question — it kills scope creep)

**If they say "I want a whole website for my business"** — gently redirect: "If I could only build one page that solves one thing, which one thing would it be?"

### Part 2 — Write a precise prompt (30 min)

Based on their answers, write a prompt that includes:

- **Who** (name, role)
- **What problem** (one sentence)
- **How they'll use it** (phone/laptop, how often)
- **The parts of the page** (fields, sections)
- **Explicitly: what the page should NOT do** (based on their answer to question 4)

### Part 3 — Build and deploy (60 min)

- Use v0.dev
- Iterate until it looks usable
- Deploy to Vercel
- **Open on phone first**, not laptop. If it doesn't work well on phone, iterate.

### Part 4 — Show them (30 min)

Send them the URL. Watch (if you can). Note:
- What did they try first?
- What confused them?
- What did they ignore?

### Part 5 — Reflection + prompt log (30-60 min)

Submit three things in Google Classroom:

**1. The URL** (must work when the instructor opens it)

**2. Your prompt log** — copy-paste every prompt you sent to v0, in order. Example:

```
Prompt 1:
Build a page for a tea shop owner that lists today's menu.
---
Prompt 2:
Add a form at the top where she can add new menu items.
---
Prompt 3:
Make the add-item form bigger. The current menu list should be at the bottom.
```

**3. Reflection** (3-5 sentences):
- Who is this for?
- What question in the interview gave you the best insight?
- What did they do when they opened the URL?
- What would you change if you built version 2?

## Rules

1. **No imagining users.** The person must exist. The instructor may ask who they are.
2. **No AI reflection.** The AI didn't do the interview. You did. Write what you learned.
3. **One page.** If your scope grows past one page, stop and cut.
4. **Prompt log is required.** It's how the instructor sees how you think, not just what you shipped.

---

## What the instructor will check

- URL works on phone
- Prompt log shows you iterated (not one perfect prompt in one shot — that's suspicious)
- Reflection mentions **specific** details about the person — not generic answers
- The mini-app actually matches what the person asked for
