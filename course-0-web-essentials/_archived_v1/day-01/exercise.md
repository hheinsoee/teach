# Day 1 — In-Class Exercise

**Goal:** By the end of today, you have a live URL on the internet. You can open it on your phone. You can send it to a friend.

---

## What you'll build

A **personal landing page** — a one-page website about you.

- A short intro (2 sentences)
- A photo (or placeholder)
- 2-3 links (email, social, portfolio — whatever you want)

**Don't overthink the design.** The goal is to get it on the internet, not to make it beautiful.

---

## Step 1 — Open v0.dev

1. Go to **https://v0.dev**
2. Sign in with Google
3. You see a prompt box in the middle of the screen

---

## Step 2 — Prompt v0

Paste something like this into the prompt box (change the parts in `[brackets]`):

```
Build a personal landing page for [YOUR NAME].

Include:
- A photo placeholder
- A 2-sentence bio: [who you are, what you do]
- 3 links: email, LinkedIn, and [one other]

Modern, minimal design. Mobile-friendly.
```

Press Enter. v0 will generate your page in about 30-60 seconds.

**If you don't like what you see** — tell v0 what to change. For example:
- "Make the background darker"
- "Make the photo bigger"
- "Add a section with my skills"

Iterate until it looks OK. **"OK" is enough. Don't aim for perfect.**

---

## Step 3 — Deploy to Vercel

1. In v0, click the **"Deploy"** button (top right)
2. Vercel asks you to sign in — use Google or GitHub
3. Give your project a name (e.g. `my-name-landing`)
4. Click **"Deploy"**
5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Vercel will show a progress bar.
6. When it finishes, you'll see a URL like `my-name-landing.vercel.app`

**That URL is your mini-app on the internet.**

---

## Step 4 — Open on your phone

1. Copy the URL
2. Send it to yourself (email, WhatsApp, Telegram — anything)
3. Open it on your phone
4. Make sure it looks OK

---

## Step 5 — Share with the person next to you

Hand your phone to a classmate. Have them open your URL on **their** phone.

**They should be able to see your mini-app** without you doing anything. That is the test — if your URL doesn't work on someone else's phone, it's not shipped yet.

---

## Step 6 — Label what you did (no AI!)

Open a notes app or a piece of paper. **Close v0 and ChatGPT.** Write down:

1. What is a **URL**? (one sentence, your own words)
2. What did **"deploy"** do? (one sentence)
3. What is a **prompt**? (one sentence)
4. Why is this a **mini-app** and not just a webpage? (one sentence)

**You will share these with the class.** It's not graded. The point is to put words to what you just did.

---

## Done when

- [ ] You have a URL ending in `.vercel.app` (or similar)
- [ ] You opened the URL on your phone
- [ ] A classmate opened your URL on their phone
- [ ] You wrote down your Label answers, without AI help
- [ ] You read your URL aloud when the instructor asked

---

## If you finish early

- Build another landing page for a friend or family member
- Try the same prompt in **Lovable.dev** — see how it looks different

**Do not spend 30 minutes making your page prettier.** That is tomorrow's work. Today's work is **ship**.
