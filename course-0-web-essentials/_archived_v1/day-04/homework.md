# Day 4 Homework — Get 2 Real People to Sign Up and Use It

## Why this homework exists

In class, you tested with fake accounts (yourself + "friend@example.com"). That's not real users. Today's homework: **get 2 real humans to sign up on your mini-app and use it.**

Why this matters: real users do things you didn't predict. They mistype. They skip steps. They send you questions you didn't anticipate. This is where **building** becomes **shipping**.

---

## What to do (3-4 hours)

### Part 1 — Make sure your Day 4 mini-app works (30 min)

- Open your URL on your phone
- Try signup, login, logout
- Fix anything broken

### Part 2 — Pick 2 real people (15 min)

Pick **2 real humans** you can message:
- A friend
- A family member
- A classmate (from outside this course — not someone else in this cohort)

**They should actually be able to use the mini-app.** If your app is a tea-shop expense tracker, don't ask your office-worker friend. Pick someone the app makes sense for.

### Part 3 — Send them the URL and ask them to use it (60 min)

Message them:

> "Hey, I built a small app that [describe what it does]. Can you try it? Sign up with any email, add 2-3 things, and tell me what you think?"

**Then watch (don't help).**

- Record what questions they ask you
- Record what confuses them
- Record what they do that surprises you

### Part 4 — Verify in Supabase (15 min)

- Open supabase.com → your project → **Authentication** → **Users**
- You should see 2 (or more) real signups — not just your test accounts
- Take a screenshot

### Part 5 — Collect short feedback (30 min)

Ask each tester 3 questions:

1. "What did you think the app does?" (shows if your description matches reality)
2. "What was confusing?" (always something)
3. "Would you use this again?" (yes / no / "maybe if...")

Write down their answers verbatim (or close).

### Part 6 — Reflection (30-45 min)

Submit:

**1. URL** (must work)

**2. Supabase screenshot** showing your 2 real signups

**3. 2 testimonials** (one per tester) — their short feedback, copy-pasted

**4. Reflection** (5-7 sentences):

- Who are your 2 testers?
- What did they think the app did vs. what you intended?
- What's the biggest confusion you saw?
- Did anyone find a bug? What was it?
- Would you change anything in the mini-app based on this feedback?

## Rules

1. **Real humans only.** Not fake emails you made. Not another student in this course. The instructor can tell.
2. **Watch, don't help.** The goal is to see what breaks for real users. If you sit next to them and explain, you learn nothing.
3. **Feedback is quotes, not summaries.** Write what they actually said, not what you think they meant.
4. **If both testers were confused by the same thing, that's not their fault — it's a bug.**
