# Pacing — 90 Minutes Per Day

Every day is 90 min. **Never longer.** Cut content when tight.

---

## Pacing status per day

| Day | Status | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `TIGHT` | GitHub setup eats time if pre-work missed |
| 2 | `FITS` | Low |
| 3 | `TIGHT` | JS "first time seeing variables" concept jump |
| 4 | `FITS` | Functions build on variables |
| 5 | `FITS` | Loops click after functions |
| 6 | `TIGHT` | DOM is foreign; events feel abstract |
| 7 | `FITS` | localStorage is simple once DOM understood |
| 8 | `FITS` | Fetch surprising but mechanical |
| 9 | `TIGHT` | **Capstone 2 begins — new repo, context shift** |
| 10 | `TIGHT` | Features load — heads-down building |
| 11 | `FITS` | Reflection-heavy, less to build |
| 12 | `SCALES WITH SIZE` | Showcase 3 min × student count |

---

## 90-min day block

| Block | Min |
|---|---|
| HW review | 10 |
| Concept intro | 10-15 |
| Instructor demo | 15 |
| Student build | 35-40 |
| Label + commit | 10-15 |

**Never cut Student Build or Label.** Cut concept intro first.

---

## Day 1 — if pre-work missed

**Risk**: 15-20 min lost to GitHub setup.

**Cut**: concept phase to 5 min. 
**Protect**: student live URL by end of class.

---

## Day 3 — JS concept jump

**Risk**: `=` vs `===`, type confusion, "what's an array?"

**Cut**: nothing. **Extend concept to 20 min** if needed. Variables are foundational.
**Compensate**: shorten HW review.

---

## Day 6 — DOM is foreign

**Risk**: `querySelector` syntax, event listener logic.

**Cut**: concept to 10 min. Jump to demo faster — DOM clicks via demo.

---

## Day 9 — Capstone 2 context switch

**Risk**: students confused why new project.

**Cut**: nothing — need all 90 min. **Help** with repo setup heavily.

---

## Day 10 — Feature density

**Risk**: too many features in 45 min.

**Reduce ambition**: 1 required feature (search) + 1 picked. Not 3.

**Cut**: concept phase. Jump to building.

---

## Day 12 — Scales linearly

| Class size | Showcase time | Fits? |
|---|---|---|
| 1-5 | 3-15 min | Easy |
| 6-10 | 18-30 min | Yes |
| 11-15 | 33-45 min | Tight; cut "what's next" to 15 min |
| 16+ | 48+ min | Doesn't fit; split group or run showcases outside class |

**Never shorter than 2 min per student**.

---

## What CAN be cut

- Concept intro length
- HW review (if nothing to show)
- "Taste of what's next" on Day 12
- Label discussion length (keep writing)

---

## What CANNOT be cut

- Day 1 live URL
- Day 3 variable writing/reading practice
- Day 6 DOM click working
- Day 9 News App skeleton
- Day 12 student presentations

---

## Instructor stress signals

If you say any of these, you're off-plan:
- "Let me explain box model" — No. Course 1.
- "Let me show you async/await properly" — No. .then() is enough.
- "Skip Label today, we're late" — No. Cut concept.
- "Finish at home" — No. Day must end with artifact.

Re-read AGENT.md principles.

---

## Homework pacing

**Target**: 3 hr/day.

If students report:
- **>4 hr regularly** → simplify research prompts
- **<2 hr regularly** → add checkpoint questions

---

## When behind schedule

Prioritize:
1. Days 1 live URL
2. Day 3 JS variables understood
3. Day 6 DOM click working
4. Day 9-12 News App shipped

Everything else is negotiable.
