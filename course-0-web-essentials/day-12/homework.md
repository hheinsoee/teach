# Day 12 (Post-Course) — Keep Apps Alive, Get More Users

**Total**: ~3-4 hours over 1 week

---

## What to do (over 1 week)

### Part 1 — Promote both apps (1 hour)

Send URLs to more people:
- Facebook / WhatsApp status
- Family group chats
- Friends outside this course

**Goal**: 10+ total real users combined (Habit Tracker + News App).

### Part 2 — Fix 1 bug + add 1 improvement to EACH app (2 hours)

Based on real user feedback:
- Habit Tracker: fix 1 + add 1
- News App: fix 1 + add 1

Push updates.

### Part 3 — Collect 5 user quotes (30 min)

Message 5 users:
1. "Did you actually use it?"
2. "Would you use it again?"
3. "What would make it better?"

Record their actual words.

### Part 4 — Final write-up (30-60 min)

Write 10-15 sentences:

1. How many real users across both apps?
2. Which app did users prefer?
3. What's the hardest thing you learned?
4. What's the most useful thing?
5. Did you fix bugs this week?
6. Will you keep these apps alive after submitting?
7. What's your plan for Course 1?
8. What would you tell a Day 1 version of yourself?

Burmese or English.
