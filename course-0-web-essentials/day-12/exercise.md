# Day 12 — Final Showcase

**Goal**: Present your News App. See what's next.

---

## Step 1 — Final check (before class)

Make sure:
- Your News App URL loads
- Features work on your phone
- You can scroll articles
- Search works

If broken, fix FAST with AI's help. First 10 min of class.

---

## Step 2 — Prepare 3-min presentation

Write on paper:
1. What your News App does (one sentence)
2. Favorite feature (why?)
3. Best user feedback (quote)
4. What you'd add next

---

## Step 3 — Present

When teacher calls you:
1. Open your News App URL on main screen
2. Scroll articles
3. Show search / filter
4. Say your 4 points

**3 minutes.** Teacher will cut off kindly.

---

## Step 4 — Watch "What's next"

Teacher shows:
- **React** — framework for big apps
- **Cursor / v0 / Lovable** — AI agents that build entire apps
- **Backend** — servers you can build

Don't try to learn these today. Just see they exist. Course 1+ territory.

---

## Step 5 — Final reflection (in class)

Open notes. Write 3 sentences:
1. Biggest thing you learned
2. Hardest moment
3. What you'll do next week

Share with partner.

---

## Step 6 — Group photo + URLs sheet

Pose for class photo. Teacher captures all URLs.

Take a screenshot of the URL list — this is your proof.

---

## Done when

- [ ] News App URL works perfectly
- [ ] You presented 3 min
- [ ] You watched "what's next"
- [ ] You have the shared URLs sheet

---

## Post-course (1 week — see [`homework.md`](homework.md))
