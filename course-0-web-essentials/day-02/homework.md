# Day 2 Homework — CSS Research + Try 2 Styles

**Total time**: ~3 hours
**Structure**: Research (1 hr) + Build (1-1.5 hr) + Reflect (0.5 hr)

---

## Part 1 — Research (1 hour)

Learn about **3 CSS properties** that you've already used (or want to use).

Pick 3 from this list:
- `color` (text color)
- `background` (background color/image)
- `font-size` (text size)
- `font-family` (font type)
- `padding` (inside spacing)
- `margin` (outside spacing)
- `border-radius` (rounded corners)
- `display` (how things lay out — block, flex, grid)

For each of your 3 properties:
- Watch a short YouTube video (or read MDN)
- Write 2 sentences in your own words — what it does, when you'd use it

**You'll submit these notes.**

---

## Part 2 — Build (1-1.5 hours)

### A. Try 2 completely different styles

Ask AI for TWO different designs of your Habit Tracker:

**Design 1**: *"Make it look like a notebook — paper background, handwritten feel"*

**Design 2**: *"Make it look like a modern app — clean, lots of white space, blue accent color"*

Save each as a separate screenshot. Pick your favorite as your "final" styles.css.

### B. Add a hover effect

Ask AI: *"When I hover my mouse over a habit, make it change color."*

Try the hover effect in your browser.

### C. Add emoji icons to habits

Ask AI: *"Add an emoji icon next to each habit — book for study, prayer hands for pray, etc."*

### D. Break and fix

Delete the `<link>` tag from your HTML. Reload. What do you see?

Then restore. **This proves what CSS does.**
