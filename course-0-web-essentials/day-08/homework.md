# Day 8 (Post-Course) — Keep Your Apps Alive, Get Real Feedback

**Total time**: ~3-4 hours across 1 week
**Why this matters**: Shipping is the beginning. Keeping it alive is where real learning happens.

---

## What to do over the week

### Part 1 — Share with 5+ real people (1 hour, spread out)

Over the week, send both URLs to:
- Family members
- Friends outside this course
- Classmates from school/work
- Online communities you're part of

**Goal**: 5+ real users on EACH app (10 users total).

### Part 2 — Watch what happens (ongoing)

- Does anyone use it more than once?
- What questions do they ask?
- What breaks?
- What do they want?

Keep a running list.

### Part 3 — Fix ONE bug + add ONE improvement to each app (2 hours)

Based on what you noticed, pick:
- Habit Tracker: fix 1 bug + add 1 small improvement
- News App: fix 1 bug + add 1 small improvement

Push updates. Live URLs update automatically.

### Part 4 — Collect 5 user quotes (30 min)

Message 5 users (across both apps). Ask:
1. "Did you use the app this week?"
2. "What was useful or not useful?"
3. "Would you use it again?"

Record their actual words.

### Part 5 — Final write-up (30 min)

Write 8-12 sentences answering:

1. How many real users did each app get?
2. Did anyone use them more than once?
3. What was the most surprising feedback?
4. What did you fix / improve this week?
5. What's the biggest difference between "it works" (Day 8) and "people actually use it" (today)?
6. Will you keep these apps alive? Why / why not?
7. What's next for you — Course 1? Self-study? New project?
8. If you could redo Course 0, what would you change?

Burmese or English.
