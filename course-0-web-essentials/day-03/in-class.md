# Day 3 — In-Class Guide

**Pre-class**: You've done prework (setup) + self-study (variables, types, console.log)

**You bring**: 3 questions from self-study, ready to ask

**Class time**: 90 min, flipped format (you do most of the talking)

---

## What happens in class today

### Phase 1 — Q&A (25 min)

**Each student shares 1 question** from self-study.

Teacher collects all questions on board/screen.

Don't worry if your question feels "stupid" — most questions are shared by many students. The silence comes from everyone thinking theirs is dumb.

**If you did self-study well, you have questions.** That's the point.

---

### Phase 2 — Discussion (15 min)

Teacher explains the most common confusions.

**Participate** — it's not a lecture. If teacher's explanation also confuses you, say so. Push back.

---

### Phase 3 — Live Building (25 min)

Teacher uses AI to build something on big screen. You follow along:

1. Open your `script.js`
2. Modify based on what teacher does
3. Push changes
4. See your URL update

**Don't just copy** — tweak what AI gives. Change variable names. Add your own logs.

---

### Phase 4 — Label (10 min)

Write (no AI, 5 min):

1. What is a **variable** in your words?
2. `let` vs `const` — when use which?
3. Name 3 data types + an example of each

Share answers with a partner.

---

### Phase 5 — Wrap + next assignment (15 min)

Teacher assigns:
- **Day 4 prework** (Viber message, coming in 2 days)
- **Day 4 self-study** (night before Day 4)
- **Today's homework** (`homework.md`)

---

## Rules in class

1. **Bring 3 questions** — it's the ticket to class
2. **Ask freely** — "I don't know" is welcome
3. **Don't apologize for questions** — every question helps the whole class
4. **Your phone is for Viber only** — not distractions during Q&A

---

## If you came without self-study

Be honest with teacher at start.

You'll:
- Sit in during Q&A (learn from others)
- Catch up during build phase (teacher helps)
- Owe extra self-study for Day 4

**Don't fake it.** Flipped classroom relies on honesty.

---

## Questions welcome anytime

- Q&A phase: formal time
- Discussion phase: interactive
- Live building: "wait, why did that work?"
- Label phase: "I still don't get X"

**Silence is OK too** — just means you're thinking.
