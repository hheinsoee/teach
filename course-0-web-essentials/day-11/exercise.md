# Day 11 — Polish + Real Users

**Goal**: Read your own News App code to understand it. Get 3 real users to try it.

---

## Part A — Read your own code

### Step 1 — Open `script.js`

Read top to bottom. Try to follow the flow.

### Step 2 — Ask AI to explain sections

Paste:

```
Explain this code section-by-section in simple English.
I want to understand what each part does, not critique it.

[PASTE YOUR script.js]
```

Read AI's explanation. Any section still unclear?

### Step 3 — Ask follow-up

For any confusing line:

```
Explain this line like I'm a complete beginner: [paste line]
```

---

## Part B — Real users (25 min)

### Step 4 — Message 3 real people

Send your News App URL to **3 real people**:
- Not classmates in this course
- Family, friends, coworkers OK

Message (adapt to your style):

> *"Hey! I built a News App for my course. Can you open on your phone and try it for 2 minutes? Tell me what you think."*

### Step 5 — Watch (if possible)

If anyone is nearby, hand them your phone and watch:
- What they tap first
- What confuses them
- What they skip

If remote, read their messages carefully.

### Step 6 — Save feedback

Screenshot their replies. You'll submit these in homework.

---

## Part C — Fix 1 thing based on feedback

Pick the most important issue from users. Fix with AI.

Push. Test.

---

## Step 7 — Label (no AI!)

Write:
1. What is **UX feedback**?
2. Why real users > self-testing?
3. Section of code you understand best?
4. Section still unclear?
5. Most surprising user reaction?

---

## Done when

- [ ] Read your entire `script.js` with AI help
- [ ] Sent URL to 3 real people
- [ ] Collected 3 feedback messages
- [ ] Fixed 1 thing based on feedback
- [ ] Label answers written

---

## Rules

1. **Real humans only.** Not classmates.
2. **Quote actual words**, not your interpretation.
3. **Negative feedback is valuable.** Write it down.
