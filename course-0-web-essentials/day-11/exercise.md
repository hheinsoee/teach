# Day 11 — Code Review + Real Users

**Goal**: Read your News App code critically. Get 3 real users to try it.

---

## Part A — Self-review (first half of class)

### Step 1 — Open your `script.js`

Read it top to bottom.

For each major section:
- What does it do?
- What could go wrong?
- Any lines you don't understand?

### Step 2 — Ask AI for review

Prompt:

```
Review this code and suggest 2 improvements:

[PASTE YOUR script.js]

Focus on:
- Error handling
- Edge cases
- Readability

Don't rewrite everything. Just suggest 2 small changes.
```

Read suggestions. Apply ones that make sense. Reject ones you don't understand.

### Step 3 — Test edge cases

Try breaking your app:
- Reload with internet off (airplane mode)
- Resize browser very small
- Search for something no article has
- Click an article with no image (if any)

For each failure, note what should happen vs what happens.

### Step 4 — Fix 1 bug

Pick ONE issue. Fix it with AI's help. Push.

---

## Part B — Real users (second half)

### Step 5 — Message 3 real people

Send your News App URL to **3 real people**:
- Not classmates in this cohort
- Family, friends, coworkers OK

Ask them (in Burmese or English):
> *"Hey, I built a News App for my course. Can you open on your phone and tell me what you think? Takes 2 min."*

### Step 6 — Collect feedback

Save their messages (screenshots).

Pay attention to:
- Did they understand what the app does?
- What was confusing?
- Would they use it again?

### Step 7 — Label (no AI!)

Write:
1. What is **code review**?
2. What is a **bug**?
3. What's **hallucination** (AI context)?
4. Why ask real users?
5. One bug you found today?

## Rules

1. **Real users only.** Not classmates.
2. **Save actual quotes.** Not what you think they meant.
3. **Even negative feedback is good.** It teaches you what to improve.
