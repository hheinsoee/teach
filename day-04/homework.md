**Google Classroom assignment**
**Points:** 10
**Due:** evening before Day 5
**Submit:** GitHub link to `notes/day-04.md`

## Task

Finish all 5 broken files from class. For each, write in `notes/day-04.md`:

1. What it was supposed to do
2. What it actually did
3. The line that was wrong
4. **Why** it was wrong — in your own words
5. Your fix

## Rules

- Find the problem yourself first. Write down your guess **before** opening the agent.
- You may use the agent to confirm, after you have written your guess.
- If your guess was wrong, write that down too. That is worth marks.

## Part 2 — The silent ones

Two of the five files produced no error message.

Answer in the same file:

1. Which two were they?
2. How did you find the problem with no error to read?
3. Why are bugs like this more dangerous than ones that crash?
