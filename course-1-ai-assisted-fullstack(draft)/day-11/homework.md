# Homework 11 — Five Real Users

**Google Classroom assignment**
**Points:** 10
**Due:** evening before Day 12
**Submit:** live URL + GitHub link + log screenshots

---

## Part 1 — Production is configured (4 points)

- [ ] Production database, separate from development
- [ ] No secrets anywhere in your GitHub repository
- [ ] Logging that records failures with enough detail to find them
- [ ] Error logging connected
- [ ] Backups switched on

## Part 2 — Get 5 real people to use it (6 points)

Five people who are not in this class. Family, friends — real people.

Ask each to sign up and add at least 3 expenses. Do not help them. Watch.

In `notes/day-11.md` record:

1. What confused them? Where did they hesitate or get stuck?
2. What errors appeared in your logs while they used it?
3. Did anyone do something you did not expect?
4. Did anything break? How did you find out — your logs, or them telling you?
5. What is the one thing you would fix first?

## Part 3 — The honest questions

1. If your app went down right now, how long until you knew?
2. If your database were deleted right now, what exactly would you do?
3. Open your logs from the last 24 hours. Can you tell what actually happened? If not, why not?

## Done when

- [ ] 5 real people have used your live app
- [ ] Their activity is visible in your logs
- [ ] All questions answered honestly, including the uncomfortable ones
- [ ] Screenshots of real log output attached

## Submit

1. Your live URL
2. GitHub link to `notes/day-11.md`
3. Screenshots of your logs showing real user activity
