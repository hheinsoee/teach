# Final Submission — The Capstone

**Google Classroom assignment**
**Points:** 10
**Due:** end of Day 12
**Submit:** live URL + repository + the written defence

---

## The app must have

- [ ] Sign up and log in that work
- [ ] Add, list, edit, delete expenses
- [ ] Filter by category and month
- [ ] Monthly total and total per category
- [ ] Deployed, live, on HTTPS
- [ ] Protected routes that return 404 for other people's data
- [ ] Logging and error logging connected
- [ ] Database backups on
- [ ] No secrets in the repository
- [ ] Tests that fail when the code is broken

## The written defence — `notes/final.md`

1. **The map.** Describe your app at each of the four layers: browser, network, server, database.
2. **The data model.** Your tables, and why each decision was made.
3. **Security.** Name the exact line that stops one user reading another's data.
4. **A rejection.** Something the agent suggested that you refused, and your reasoning.
5. **A failure.** Something that broke in production, how you found out, how you fixed it.
6. **The weakest part.** Which part of your app do you understand least?

Question 6 is graded on honesty, not on the answer. Everyone has one. A builder who
knows where their blind spot is will survive; one who claims there is none will not.

## How this is graded

The app being live is **one** of six criteria. The others happen in the oral exam:
explaining your own code, finding a bug without the agent, spotting bad agent output,
and defending your data model.

A perfect app you cannot explain does not pass. You were told this in day 1.
