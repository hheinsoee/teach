# Homework 5 — Ten Reviewed Commits

**Google Classroom assignment**
**Points:** 10
**Due:** evening before Day 6
**Submit:** GitHub link to your commit history

---

## Part 1 — Ten commits (6 points)

Make **10 commits** to your repository. Each one:

- is a single small change
- has a message that explains **why**, not what
- was reviewed with `git diff` before committing

Good message: `use integer cents for amount so totals do not lose money`
Bad message: `update`, `fix`, `changes`, `asdf`

## Part 2 — The review log (4 points)

In `notes/day-05.md`, document **three** of those commits:

1. What you asked the agent for
2. What the diff actually changed — files, added lines, deleted lines
3. Anything you rejected, and why
4. Did it change anything you did not expect?

Then answer:

> You asked for one thing and the diff changed 4 files. Is that fine or is it a
> warning sign? Explain your reasoning.

## Done when

- [ ] 10 commits pushed, each with a real message
- [ ] No commit is larger than you can read in 2 minutes
- [ ] `notes/day-05.md` documents 3 reviews
- [ ] You rejected at least one thing the agent produced

If you rejected nothing all week, you are not reviewing — you are scrolling.
