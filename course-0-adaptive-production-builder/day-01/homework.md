# Day 1 Homework — Ship Another Mini-App for Someone Else

## Why this homework exists

Today you shipped a mini-app for **yourself**. Shipping for yourself is easy — you know what you want.

Shipping for **someone else** is harder — you have to understand what they need. That is the real builder's skill. This homework starts building it.

---

## What to do (2 hours, max)

### Part 1 — Pick a person (10 min)

Pick someone in your life who could use a simple one-page website:

- A family member who runs a small business
- A friend who wants to showcase their work
- A classmate who has a hobby/project
- Someone selling something on Facebook who has no website

**They do not need to know you're doing this.** Just pick someone whose situation you understand.

### Part 2 — Decide what the page will say (15 min)

Write down, on paper or notes:
- **Who is this for?** (name, what they do)
- **What should the page tell people?** (3-5 pieces of info)
- **What should visitors do after they read it?** (call? message? follow?)

### Part 3 — Build it with v0.dev (60 min)

Use what you learned in class. Prompt v0, iterate until it looks OK, deploy to Vercel.

**New constraint:** this time your prompt should mention **the person** and **the purpose**. For example:

```
Build a landing page for a Burmese tea shop owner named U Myint.

The page should tell customers:
- Shop name and tagline
- Where the shop is (address + Google Maps link)
- What's on the menu (3-4 items)
- Opening hours
- A phone number to call for orders

Design: warm, friendly, slightly traditional feel.
```

### Part 4 — Open on phone, share URL (15 min)

- Copy the URL
- Open it on your phone
- Send the URL to the person you built it for (**or**, if you don't want to send it yet, send it to one other friend)
- Make sure they can open it

### Part 5 — Reflection (20 min)

Write 3-5 sentences answering:

1. **Who was this for?**
2. **What did your first prompt say?** (copy-paste it)
3. **What did you change after you saw the first result?**
4. **What was the hardest part?**
5. **Would the person actually use this?**

In Burmese or English — your choice.

## Rules

1. **Don't ask AI to write your reflection.** The reflection is for you to notice what you learned. If AI writes it, you learned nothing.
2. **One mini-app, not three.** This is practice at scoping small. If you're tempted to build "a whole website with 5 pages", stop — pick one page.
3. **Ugly ships beat beautiful drafts.** A URL that works and looks mediocre is better than a design that never left your laptop.
